Weekly challenge: survive the crash

Everyone gets the same rough patch from the past and the same pretend money. Lose less than the market and you survive.

 

1. This week’s rough patch

The market is about to have a bad few months. You have $10,000 of pretend money. You will not be told when it happens, only that prices fall. Choose what to hold, or keep some cash. Then we replay it and compare you with the S&P 500 fund, which holds 500 big companies.

Cash earns nothing, but it does not fall either.
