Who this is for

Build log

  • M7 · Move to a real app 4 November 2026

    The whole app moved to Next.js and now lives on Vercel, the stack real startups use. Every page looks and works the same, and the old GitHub Pages link forwards to the new site so no link breaks. All tests pass.

  • M6 · Learn mode 1 November 2026

    Five guided missions played inside the real app. Each one dims the screen and lights up one control, and the Continue button only unlocks once you have really done the step (grow the calculator to 20 years, buy an index fund, see a risky score turn green, tap the biggest one-day drop). The same six-question quiz runs before and after, so you see what you learned. It uses its own practice wallet, so it never touches your real pretend portfolio. All tests pass.

  • M5 · Risk and diversification 25 October 2026

    The trade page now shows where your money is (a ring split by asset type, sector and currency), how bumpy it is (how much your shares move on a normal day, in rupees, from six months of real prices), and a diversification score from 0 to 100 with one gentle tip. Tested: one stock scores 0, five stocks 57, a mix with an index fund 94, the same every time.

  • M4 · Paper trading core 19 October 2026 · tested 20 October

    Buy and sell the 20 instruments with ₹1,00,000 of pretend money, part shares allowed. Money is kept in whole paise so cash plus holdings always equals the total. No borrowing, no short selling. Sahil made 20 trades: no glitches, and it added up every time. Updated 25 October: ₹10,00,000 to start, whole shares only, and one buy can be at most a quarter of your total.

  • M3 · Real market data, refreshed by itself 12 October 2026 · checked 19 October

    Exchange rates and 20 instruments with real closing prices, fetched nightly by a GitHub Action. The API key lives in GitHub Secrets, never in the code. It then ran six nights in a row on its own, with nobody touching it.

  • M2 · Compound interest calculator 12 October 2026

    Sliders for your plan, a growth chart, and the cost of waiting five years. The maths sits in its own file with five tests that run from the terminal.
