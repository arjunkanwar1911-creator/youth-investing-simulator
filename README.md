# Youth Investing Simulator

**Live link:** https://youth-investing-simulator.vercel.app

**Progress:** M7 of 16 milestones done · now building M8, accounts with Google sign in ([`PROGRESS.md`](PROGRESS.md))

Learn how money grows — compound interest, risk and diversification — by investing
pretend money on real market prices.

> Educational, pretend money, not financial advice.

## Who it's for, and why

Most 14-to-18 year olds have been told "invest early" and have never seen what that
actually does. Advice is cheap; the feeling of watching 8% a year compound for ten years
is what changes behaviour.

This app gives that feeling without the risk:

- **A 14-to-18 year old** who wants to understand investing before they have money to lose.
- **Someone who learns by doing** — make the mistakes here, where they cost nothing.
- **A teacher or youth club** who needs a safe, free way to teach investing in one lesson.

Success isn't the app. It's how many young people learn to invest responsibly with it.

## Rules this project keeps

- Pretend money only. No real trading, no bank or broker links, ever.
- Free plans only. Nothing that needs a credit card.
- Daily closing prices, not live tick data.
- Privacy and safety before growth — it's built for young learners.

## Built with

- **App:** Next.js, hosted on Vercel (free Hobby plan)
- **Charts:** Chart.js
